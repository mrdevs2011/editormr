EMR ("we", "the app") is a browser-based video editor. This page explains what data the app handles and why. It is written to be short and plain.

1. What we collect

When you sign in we receive only what is needed to identify your account:

  • Google sign-in: your email address, name, and profile picture URL, as provided by Google.
  • Email sign-in: the email address you type in.

We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data.

2. Your projects and media

Your projects, videos, images, and audio are uploaded to our cloud storage (Supabase) so your projects stay available across the devices you sign in on. They are not kept only in your browser — access to them is tied to your account. If this changes, we will update this page before it goes live.

3. How we use data

  • To let you sign in and keep you signed in.
  • To show your email in the app and to contact you about your account if needed.

We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads or use your data for advertising.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase — authentication and account storage.
  • Google — optional "Sign in with Google".
  • Vercel — website hosting.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

5. Cookies and local storage

The app uses your browser's local storage to keep your sign-in session and to store your projects. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Deleting your data

You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and the account data we hold, email us at the address below and we will remove it. Local projects can be removed by clearing site data in your browser.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Anonymous usage and error logs

To improve reliability we may store anonymous events and client error reports in our database:

  • What is stored: event names such as project_created, export_started, export_completed, export_failed, onboarding_completed, pwa_installed (if available); approximate time; optional non-identifying metadata (e.g. clip count, quality label).
  • What is not stored: your email, IP address, file names, video content, project titles, or any cookie that tracks you across sessions for advertising.
  • Errors: browser error message and a shortened stack trace (paths stripped), page URL path — so we can fix crashes. Duplicate messages in the same tab are rate-limited.
  • Deletion: because these records are not tied to your account identity, there is nothing personal to “delete” for a specific user. Data is used only for aggregate reliability.

No third-party analytics scripts (Google Analytics, Sentry, etc.) are loaded. Data stays in our Supabase project with insert-only access from the app.
